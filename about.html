About Braeside Dev

A small studio building apps that respect your privacy

Who We Are

Braeside Dev LLC is an independent software studio focused on building practical, privacy-respecting mobile applications. We are a small team of developers who believe that useful software doesn't need to come at the cost of your personal data.

Our Approach

Every app we build starts from a simple question: can this work without collecting user data? More often than not, the answer is yes. Modern devices are powerful enough to handle most tasks locally, and peer-to-peer networking can replace cloud infrastructure for many use cases.

We design our apps to be self-contained. That means no mandatory accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party trackers. When network communication is necessary, we use end-to-end encryption and peer-to-peer connections wherever possible.

What We Value

Privacy by Default

We don't collect, store, or transmit personal data. Your information stays on your devices.

Simplicity

Our apps do one thing well. No feature bloat, no upsells, no dark patterns.

Transparency

Clear privacy policies, honest descriptions, and no hidden data collection.

Fair Pricing

One-time purchases where possible. We build sustainable software without subscription fatigue.

Based In

Braeside Dev LLC is a United States-based company.

EarHorn
Turn two iPhones into a secure baby monitor. EarHorn streams audio peer-to-peer over your home WiFi — no cloud servers, no accounts, no subscriptions.
iOS Privacy-First Peer-to-Peer

Key Features

  • Peer-to-peer audio — streams directly between devices over your local WiFi network
  • End-to-end encryption — audio is encrypted with P-256 elliptic curve cryptography
  • No cloud dependency — works without internet once devices are paired
  • No account required — no sign-up, no email, no personal information collected
  • No subscription — one-time purchase, no recurring fees
  • Great for travel — works anywhere with a WiFi network, no extra hardware to pack

Learn more at earhorn.app.
